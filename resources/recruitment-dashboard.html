The takeaway

From 2022 to 2026: ~3,300 CVs and ~130 hires, about 4% overall. Volume nearly doubled at the 2025 peak while the hire rate drifted from ~6% to ~3%. More CVs did not mean more hires. Referrals were ~6% of CVs but ~30% of hires, and most rejections happen at CV and hiring-manager screen. The work since then: automated intake to handle volume, tighter JDs and screening to reduce noise, and more referral and direct sourcing where quality matters.

Source: internal recruitment tracker, aggregated and rounded. "Direct email" = applications sent to the recruitment inbox. Location = candidate's stated location. 2026 covers Jan–Oct.
