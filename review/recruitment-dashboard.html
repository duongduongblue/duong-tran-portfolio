Key takeaways

Four shifts that define the period.

Inbound replaced LinkedInLinkedIn fell from 54% to 7% of CVs; direct email rose from 2% to 85% (2022 → 2026). Job boards stopped after 2023.
Referrals: few CVs, many hiresAbout 6% of CVs but 31% of hires. In 2022, 18 of 34 hires came by referral.
Role mix moved to Art & Game DesignFrom 11% of CVs in 2022 to 59% in 2025; 36 of 59 hires in 2025–26.
Freeze, then rebuild, in one city7 hires in 2023, then 29 and 40. By 2026, 98% of CVs and every hire are in Ho Chi Minh City.

What this shows

  • Source effectiveness, measured to hire. Tracking each source through to hire showed referrals converting at ~20% against ~4% overall, and inbound email growing to 85% of CVs, cutting reliance on LinkedIn and paid job boards.
  • Hiring that follows the business. Headcount growth went from a near freeze (7 hires) to 40 hires within two years, while the role mix shifted to Art & Game Design and hiring moved to one office.
  • Data-driven reporting from a spreadsheet. 3,315 CV records across five yearly tabs were consolidated into one dataset, so funnel conversion, source and role trends can be compared year on year.

Data notes

  • Time-to-hire is not tracked: start and offer dates were not recorded consistently.
  • 2026 covers January to October.
  • 281 CVs have no final status, so conversion rates are minimums.
  • Role groups are mapped from job titles. Shares are rounded; “Other” is the remainder.
