Operations handover

This is the day-to-day office work for the Đà Nẵng and HCM offices. The Hà Nội office closed in early 2026, so there's nothing left to do there.

Each month

  • Start of the month: open a new asset tab for each office and check which contracts are about to expire.
  • By the 10th: collect the invoices from the regular office vendors and send them for payment.
  • End of the month: compare who joined and who left against the parking and asset lists, then ask everyone to confirm the equipment they hold.

Parking

  • When someone new needs a parking card, get their name, plate number and start date and send them to the building management team. Once the card works, add it to that month's parking list.
  • When someone leaves, tell the building to cancel the card and remove it from the list the same day.

Assets

  • Giving out equipment: hand it over with a signed form and add it to the current month's tab.
  • Taking it back: when someone leaves, collect their equipment on their last day and put it back in admin storage.
  • Moving it between offices: mark it as moved in the old office's file and add it to the new office's file, noting where it came from.
  • Monthly check: each person confirms their list in a direct message, and you note the date.

Vendors

  • Rent, utilities, cleaning and water are billed every month, mostly around the 10th. Plants are billed every quarter, and courier only when it's used.
  • Payment steps are on the payment request page.

Where things live

  • Asset files and parking lists are in Google Sheets, and the full handover notes are in Notion.
  • Requests and confirmations from the team come in on Slack. The building team is on Zalo.
