How the day-to-day office work runs at the Đà Nẵng and HCM offices: what happens each month, the steps for each task, and where the files are kept. Written so whoever picks this up can follow it without having to ask.

02 · Monthly rhythm / Nhịp hằng tháng

Three checkpoints each month

Stick to these three dates and most problems get caught before anyone notices them.

D1
Start of month

Open the new month

Copy last month's asset tab into a new tab for each office, then check whether any contract is close to its end date.

D10
By the 10th

Close recurring vendor items

Collect the monthly invoices from recurring vendors and send them on for payment.

D28
End of month

Reconcile and confirm

Check this month's joiners and leavers against the parking and asset lists, and ask each holder to confirm their equipment.

03 · Building & parking / Tòa nhà & gửi xe

Stay in touch with the building

Each office has a building management group on Zalo. That group is where requests go.

Parking card flow

04 · Assets / Tài sản

Every device has one holder and one row

Each office has one tab per month. Whenever a device changes hands, write it down.

1

Hand out equipment

Prepare the standard setup for the role, have the new holder sign the handover form, and add the device to this month's tab.

Open task guide · Issue equipment
2

Take equipment back

When someone leaves, fill in the return form and put the device back in admin storage the same day.

Open task guide · Return equipment
3

Move equipment between offices

Mark the row as moved in the sending office's file, then add it to the receiving office's file with a note saying where it came from.

Open task guide · Office transfer
4

Confirm with holders

At month end, send each holder their list by Slack DM. Once they confirm, note the date.

Open task guide · Holder confirmation
05 · Test devices & office care

Count the test devices with the people who use them

Test devices are checked together with the Đà Nẵng team. Admin looks after office supplies.

Test device check

Sit down with the team and go through the list one device at a time.

  • Check every device against the list.
  • Note the date of the check.
  • Write down anything missing or broken, and who is following it up.

Office supplies & wifi cabinet

Small things that make the office run smoothly every day.

  • Keep basic supplies stocked and reorder before they run out.
  • Check the wifi cabinet regularly and keep it tidy.
  • Report building issues through the building group.
