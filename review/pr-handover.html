01 · Basics

What is a PR?

A payment request asks for approval to pay an office expense.

It includes

  • A payment form
  • Invoices and supporting documents
  • The contract, if there is one

Two types

  • With contract
  • Without contract
02 · Flow

Six steps per PR

02Fill the formOne A4 page, signed by the requester.

05 · Tips

Quick tips

Small habits that prevent rework.

Before sending

  • Amounts and dates match the form
  • Documents complete and in order
  • File names follow the convention

Always

  • Check tracking first
  • Ask, don’t guess
  • Note special cases briefly
