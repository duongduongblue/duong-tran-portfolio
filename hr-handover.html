02 · Recruitment / Tuyển dụng

Move every candidate forward with a visible next step

The flow may flex by role, but the handoff points and records should stay clear.

General hiring flow

Screening → Offer
01Screening CVCheck against the JD criteria.
02Phone interviewCollect role-specific information.
03HM reviewShare the candidate summary.
04TestUse the relevant test and deadline.
05Interview R1Technical team or livecode.
06Interview R2CEO round where applicable.
07FeedbackFollow up promptly.
08OfferAlign, approve, communicate.

Game Designer

Screening CV → Phone Interview → HM Review → Test (2 weeks) → Interview R1 (Technical Team) → Interview R2 (CEO).

Technical / Software

Screening CV → Phone Interview → HM Review → Test (2 weeks) → Livecode + Interview (Technical Team) → Interview R2 (CEO).

1

Receive the hiring request

Prepare the JD using the template, send it to the Hiring Manager for review and revision, then upload the approved JD to HRM and choose the appropriate sourcing channels.

Open task guide · Master Job Description
2

Screen the CV

Review each CV against the agreed JD criteria. Keep the screening decision consistent and explainable.

Open task guide · Candidate screening
3

Store and track candidate data

Record candidate information in the tracking file. Create a new tracker or use an automated intake tool when the hiring volume requires it.

Open task guide · Candidate Tracking
4

Run the phone interview

Use the call to gather the information needed for the specific role, then summarize the conversation and send it to the HM.

Open task guide · Phone interview
5

Send and follow up on the test

Choose the right test, communicate the deadline, monitor submission and send reminders when needed.

Open task guide · Technical Test
6

Coordinate interview / livecode

Arrange the session with the HM. Before the meeting, send the Interview Note so interviewers can capture feedback in one place.

Open task guide · Interview Notes
7

Chase feedback and propose offer

Follow up with interviewers as early as possible. If the candidate passes, align the offer with the HRM before submitting it to the CEO for approval.

Open task guide · Feedback and offer
8

Communicate with the candidate

Discuss the agreed terms with the candidate and send the Offer Letter once approvals and details are complete.

Open task guide · Offer Letter
03 · People lifecycle / Vòng đời nhân sự

Onboarding is a sequence, not a single day

Start one week before the join date so people, tools and space are ready together.

D-7
One week before

Set up access and workplace readiness

Create the new member's email in Google Admin Console and GitHub account using the format FirstNameLastNameRoleAther. Coordinate equipment and workspace needs with Operations and IT.

Open task guide · Account setup
D-5
3–5 days before

Make the arrival visible

Post the pre-onboarding notice in Slack #pre-onboarding. Share the new member information with the relevant onboarding channel and confirm the responsible people are aware.

  • Game Designer: #game-designer-onboarding-it-leader-admin
  • Art: #art-team-onboarding-it-leader-admin
  • Game QA / Producer: #game-qa-producer-onboard-it-leader-hr-admin
  • Game Engineer: #game-engineer-onboard-it-leader-hr-admin
Open task guide · Onboarding communication
D-2
Before joining

Close the practical details

Register the parking card in the relevant Zalo group based on candidate information. Update the HCM seating map when needed. Prepare the Welcome / Orientation deck and the new-hire introduction visual.

  • Parking card tracker: HCM and Đà Nẵng Google Sheets
  • Seating Map: Google Slides
  • Welcome Onboard Orientation: Google Slides
  • New Hire Introduction: Canva

Offboarding

Prepare the required offboarding information and documents using the prescribed form. Coordinate with chị Trúc, Operations and all relevant stakeholders.

  • Use the required offboarding form.
  • Coordinate the handoff with Trúc and Operations.
  • Confirm all involved parties have the information they need.
Open task guide · Offboarding

Probation tracking

Protect the deadline by starting early. Send the evaluation form and reminder at least two weeks before the probation review is due.

  • Track the employee and Line Manager completion status.
  • Use Employee Tracking in Google Sheets.
  • Use the Probation Notification Email Template in Notion.

Reporting cadence

Keep work visible and easy to pick up.

  • Reference the Daily Standup Report example in Notion.

04 · EE / EB / IC

Plan the moments that keep people connected

Employee Engagement, Employer Branding and Internal Communications should be planned as a steady rhythm, not only as one-off events.

How to work the calendar

Maintain the communication plan, note the social post content, then build a monthly or annual timeline for execution. Use the existing recurring programs as the starting point and adapt them to the year's needs.
