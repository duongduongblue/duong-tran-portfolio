## Overview

This handover covers recurring operations and people-support work across office administration, procurement, payment requests, contracts, onboarding, offboarding, internal events, and ad-hoc support.

Main work groups:

1. Office and building management
2. Office procurement, equipment, and pantry supplies
3. Cost tracking and payment requests / DNTT / PR
4. Contracts, invoices, mail, and records
5. New employee onboarding
6. Employee offboarding
7. Internal events and internal communication
8. Ad-hoc tasks such as BOD support, business trips, repairs, visitors, and access cards
9. Office asset tracking

### Standard Flow

```text

↓

├─ Tealady / cleaning support
```

## C. Cost Management And Payment Requests / DNTT / PR

This is one of the most important workstreams because it affects money, approvals, and accounting records.

### General Rules

- Administrative costs should be submitted early in the week where possible.
- Normal administrative payments may take several working days.
- Contract-based payments may take longer because they require more review.
- New equipment purchases such as PC, laptop, SSD, or similar items should include handover records where needed.

### C1. General Flow

```text
Cost appears
↓
Check whether prior approval is required
├─ Yes: new cost, large amount, outside budget, or advance payment needed
└─ No: recurring weekly/monthly cost
↓
Purchase goods or receive service
↓
Collect supporting documents
↓
Prepare DNTT / payment request
↓
Review
↓
Submit to accounting
↓
Track payment status
```

### C2. When Prior Approval Is Required

Approval is required for:

- New costs
- Costs not included in budget
- High-value costs
- Cases requiring advance payment from accounting

Approval is usually not required for recurring or routine small costs such as:

- Weekly snacks and drinks
- Stationery
- Small administrative supplies
- Recurring monthly office costs such as cleaning, office lease, water, tissue, trash bags, and coffee

### C3. DNTT Classification

#### Type 1: DNTT With Contract

Used for:

- Vendors with signed contracts
- Recurring services
- Large purchases that require formal terms
- VAT-supported expenses

Examples:

- Cleaning
- Drinking water
- Office lease
- Courier service
- High-value computer, laptop, or furniture purchase

#### Type 2: DNTT Without Contract

Used for:

- Regular administrative costs
- VAT-supported purchases that do not require contract

Examples:

- Snacks and drinks
- Stationery
- Small components
- Low-value retail purchases

#### Non-VAT Or Informal Receipt Cases

Used for:

- Costs without VAT invoice
- Small purchases from retail platforms or local shops

### C4. Standard DNTT Process

```text
Purchase goods / receive service
↓
Record information in payment request tracking file
↓
Collect supporting documents
├─ Quotation
├─ VAT invoice
├─ Contract / appendix
└─ Delivery / acceptance / handover record
↓
Create DNTT using template
↓
Merge supporting documents into one PDF
↓
Review the document set
↓
Send for internal checking
↓
Upload to the right system/entity
↓
Submit to payment request channel
↓
Print hard copy for accounting records if required
↓
Track payment until completed
```

## D. Contracts, Invoices, Mail, And Records

### D1. Vendor Contract Handling

```text
Need for new contract or renewal appears
↓
Negotiate discount / better terms before contract step
↓
Receive draft contract from vendor or prepare draft internally
↓
Send to Legal for review and Accounting for payment term check
↓
Discuss revisions with vendor if needed
↓
Vendor signs first where preferred
↓
Company reviews, signs, initials pages, and stamps as required
↓
Send one signed copy back to vendor
↓
Scan and save contract folder
↓
Track renewal deadline monthly
```

Notes:

- Lower-value contracts may follow a lighter approval path depending on delegation rules.
- Higher-value contracts usually require formal representative approval and may take longer.
- Review upcoming contract expiry dates at the start of each month.

### D2. Incoming And Outgoing Mail

```text
Receive outgoing request or incoming mail
↓
Check sender / receiver details
↓
Pack documents if needed
↓
Fill postal or courier form
↓
Apply the right company stamp if required
↓
Hand over to courier
↓
Save receipt by month
```

For incoming mail:

- Receive at reception.
- Send to the right recipient.
- For inter-office mail, inform the relevant internal group.

## E. New Employee Onboarding

New joiners should have the required:

- Seat
- Computer
- Monitor
- Accessories
- Software
- Parking / access card
- Asset handover record

### E1. Onboarding Flow

```text
Receive onboarding information from HR
↓
Check work location, start date, and team
↓
Confirm equipment standard
├─ Laptop or desktop
├─ Number of monitors
├─ SSD / RAM upgrade if needed
└─ Required software
↓
Arrange seating plan
↓
Contact IT for setup
↓
Confirm configuration with line manager
↓
Prepare parking card / access card
↓
Onboarding day: hand over asset and sign record
↓
Update asset file and onboarding checklist
```

### E2. Basic Equipment Rules

- Technical roles may receive laptop or desktop plus monitor depending on request and role needs.
- Back-office roles usually receive laptop and monitor based on request.
- IT formats and prepares machines before handover.
- Confirm configuration with IT and line manager before final setup.
- Higher-than-standard hardware configurations should be escalated for approval.

## I. Office Asset Tracking

Asset tracking files should cover assets for each office location, such as:

- Laptop / desktop
- Monitor
- Mouse and keyboard
- SSD and RAM
- Other IT equipment
- Assets assigned to employees
- Assets in storage / waiting for assignment / returned

Purpose:

- Keep asset records clean.
- Support onboarding and offboarding.
- Reduce missing equipment risk.
- Help IT, HR, and Admin coordinate handover clearly.
