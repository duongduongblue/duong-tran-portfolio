Workflow Logic

How Requests Should Move

The operating pattern is simple by design. Before jumping into execution, the task should be classified, checked for required information, routed to the right support point, and tracked until completion.

Intake
Receive the request or identify a recurring need. Clarify what is being requested, where it applies, the urgency, and whether approval is required.
Classification
Group the task under office/building, procurement, payment, contracts, onboarding, offboarding, events, ad-hoc support, or asset tracking.
Coordination
Contact the correct support point such as building management, IT, vendor, HR, line manager, accounting, or legal.
Execution
Complete the task, collect required documents, prepare forms or records, and check whether follow-up action is needed.
Tracking
Save evidence, update the tracking file, and keep following until the task is complete rather than stopping at submission.
Workstreams

Main Areas Of Work

Each workstream below summarizes the task purpose and the practical checks that make the handover usable.

A · Office

Office And Building Management

Keep the office operating smoothly across electricity, water, AC, meeting rooms, pantry, plants, cleaning, access cards, and visitor areas.

B · Procurement

Office Procurement / Pantry / Equipment

  • Weekly snacks and drinks
  • Stationery and shared office supplies
  • Electronics, furniture, and equipment
  • Hardware checks with IT before purchase
C · Payment

Cost Management And Payment Requests

Track costs, check approval needs, collect supporting documents, prepare DNTT / payment requests, submit to accounting, and follow payment status.

D · Records

Contracts, Invoices, Mail, And Records

Handle vendor contracts, renewal tracking, invoice records, outgoing mail, incoming mail, scans, and filing discipline.

E · Onboarding

New Employee Onboarding

  • Confirm work location, start date, team, and equipment standard
  • Arrange seat, laptop or desktop, monitor, accessories, software, and access cards
  • Coordinate IT setup and asset handover records
F · Offboarding

Employee Offboarding

Collect assets, confirm data handover with leaders, scan records, coordinate IT reset, cancel access, and update tracking files.

G · Events

Internal Events And Communication

Plan employee engagement events, prepare ideas, budget, timeline, vendors, internal content, post-event records, and communication materials.

H · Ad-hoc

BOD Support, Business Trips, Guests, And Access Cards

Support travel research and booking, guest welcoming, meeting room preparation, access cards, repairs, urgent requests, and other operational needs.

I · Assets

Office Asset Tracking

Track laptops, desktops, monitors, accessories, IT equipment, assigned assets, returned assets, and stock waiting for assignment.

Controls

Important Operating Checks

These controls help reduce missing records, payment delays, unclear ownership, and handover gaps.

Payment Evidence

Payment requests should include quotation, VAT invoice if applicable, contract or appendix if needed, and delivery / acceptance / handover records.

Approval Logic

Check approval needs before purchase, especially for new costs, high-value items, out-of-budget expenses, or advance payment cases.

Contract Renewal

Review expiring contracts at the start of each month and leave enough time for vendor, legal, accounting, and signer review.

Hardware Setup

For laptops, desktops, RAM, SSD, VGA, CPU, and similar hardware, check with IT before purchase or handover.

Offboarding Reset

Do not reset machines until the leader confirms that work data has been handed over and admin is allowed to proceed.

Tracking Discipline

The task is not closed when submitted. It closes when payment, handover, filing, or follow-up has been fully completed.
