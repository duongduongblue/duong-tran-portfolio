Key takeaways

Four shifts that define the period.

Inbound replaced LinkedInLinkedIn fell from 51% to 8% of CVs; direct email rose from 2% to 84% (2022 → 2026). Job boards stopped after 2023.
Referrals: few CVs, many hiresAbout 7% of CVs but 29% of hires. In 2022, 12 of 26 hires came by referral.
Role mix moved to Art & Game DesignFrom 12% of CVs in 2022 to 55% in 2025; 42 of 69 hires in 2025–26.
Freeze, then rebuild, in one city10 hires in 2023, then 37 and 54. By 2026, 97% of CVs and every hire are in Ho Chi Minh City.

Year by year

Conversion = hires ÷ CVs.

  • 2022687 CVs · 26 hires · 3.8%

    Three offices hiring (Da Nang, HCM, Hanoi); referrals made 12 hires.

  • 2023434 CVs · 10 hires · 2.3%

    Similar volume, hiring nearly paused. Job boards used for the last time.

  • 2024699 CVs · 37 hires · 5.3%

    Hiring restarted. Direct email reached 39% of CVs; a test step was added to the funnel.

  • 20251,115 CVs · 54 hires · 4.8%

    Peak year for both. Art & Game Design was 55% of CVs and 33 hires.

  • 2026395 CVs · 15 hires · 3.8% (to Oct)

    All 2026 hiring focused in Ho Chi Minh City.

What this shows

  • Source effectiveness, measured to hire. Tracking each source through to hire showed referrals converting far above the overall rate (18.7% vs 4.3%), while inbound email replaced most LinkedIn and job-board sourcing.
  • Hiring that follows the business. From a near freeze in 2023 to a peak in 2025, while the role mix shifted to Art & Game Design and hiring moved to one office.
  • Data-driven reporting from a spreadsheet. Five yearly candidate tabs were consolidated into one dataset, so funnel conversion, source and role trends can be compared year on year.
