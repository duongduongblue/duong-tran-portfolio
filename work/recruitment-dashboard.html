Key takeaways

Four shifts that define the period.

Inbound replaced LinkedInLinkedIn fell from 33% to 5% of CVs; direct email rose from 3% to 58% (2022 → 2026). Job boards stopped after 2023.
Referrals: few CVs, many hiresAbout 10% of CVs but 19% of hires. In 2022, 5 of 16 hires came by referral.
Role mix moved to Art & Game DesignFrom 16% of CVs in 2022 to 72% in 2025; 34 of 41 hires in 2025–26.
Freeze, then rebuild, in one city5 hires in 2023, then 23 and 33. By 2026, 68% of CVs and 5 of 8 hires are in Ho Chi Minh City.

What this shows

  • Source effectiveness, measured to hire. Tracking each source through to hire showed referrals converting far above the overall rate (8.0% vs 4.3%), while inbound email replaced most LinkedIn and job-board sourcing.
  • Hiring that follows the business. From a near freeze in 2023 to a peak in 2025, while the role mix shifted to Art & Game Design and hiring moved to one office.
  • Data-driven reporting from a spreadsheet. Five yearly candidate tabs were consolidated into one dataset, so funnel conversion, source and role trends can be compared year on year.
