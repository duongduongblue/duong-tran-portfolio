What this shows

  • Pipeline tracking. A dashboard can follow CVs through to hire so the funnel is easy to review.
  • Comparable views. Year and month filters keep source, role and status in one place.
  • Reporting from a spreadsheet. Records can be consolidated so conversion and mix can be reviewed over time.

Data notes

  • Illustrative sample data. Figures are anonymized and do not reflect actual company numbers.
  • 2026 covers January to October.
  • Monthly counts are a sample split of each year's totals. 2026 stops at October.
  • Days to hire, interviews and offers are sample figures. Joined equals hires, and each funnel stage is at most the stage before it.
  • November and December 2026 are not yet in the sample, so those months stay blank.
  • Some CVs have no final status, so conversion rates are minimums.
  • Role groups are mapped from job titles. Categories absent in a year are not listed.
Aggregates only. No candidate data is shown.
