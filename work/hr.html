02 · Recruitment

Every candidate has a clear next step

Steps flex by role. The CEO round is optional for some roles.

CV screening→Phone screen→Hiring manager review→Test (2 weeks)→Round 1: team→Round 2: CEO→Feedback→Offer

Game Designer: Round 1 is an interview with the team. Engineering: Round 1 is a live-coding session plus interview.

01

Open the role

  • Draft the JD from the template.
  • Get hiring manager sign-off.
  • Post on the HRM system and job channels.
02

Screen and track

  • Screen CVs against the JD.
  • Log every candidate in the tracker.
  • Use a form for high-volume roles.
03

Phone screen and test

  • Send a short summary to the hiring manager.
  • Send the right test with a clear deadline.
  • Remind before the due date.
04

Interview and offer

  • Share the interview note before each round.
  • Collect feedback quickly.
  • Agree the offer with HRM, get CEO approval, send the letter.
03 · Employee lifecycle

Onboarding starts a week early

People, tools and desk should be ready on day one.

D-7

Accounts and desk

  • Create company email and work accounts.
  • Prepare laptop and desk with Operations and IT.
D-5

Tell the team

  • Post the pre-onboarding note.
  • Brief each team's onboarding group.
D-2

Final prep

  • Register parking and update the seating plan.
  • Prepare welcome slides and intro image.
Day 1

Welcome

  • Send the welcome email.
  • Run the orientation session.
  • Introduce the new joiner company-wide.

Offboarding

  • Use the offboarding template.
  • Coordinate the exit with Operations.

Probation

  • Send review forms 2 weeks ahead.
  • Follow up with employee and manager.
  • Update the employee tracker.

Reporting

  • Daily: post today's tasks each morning.
  • Weekly: update the weekly report.

Records

  • New joiners and leavers by year.
  • Probation tracker.
