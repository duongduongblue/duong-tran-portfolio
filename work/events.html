02 · Playbook per event type

Every event follows Pre / In / Post

Budget model

HR line: per-head, in VND, scaled to the event type .
Admin / Operation line: per office, in USD, with an optional contingency line.

Standard roles

HR lead: overall plan, budget, posts, summary. Event support: decor, gifts, MC script, photos, attendee lists, split by office. Leadership approval: recognitions and shoutouts (leaders choose, HR compiles, CEO approves).

Company Trip

Annual · 3 days 2 nights

Key steps

  • Pre Theme + dates (2026: “A-STAR”), vendor contract
  • Pre Logistics: participant list, buses, room-sharing
  • In Teambuilding + gala night
  • Post Recap on internal and social channels
Budget modelHR per-head VND line (largest of all events) + Admin per-office USD line.
RolesHR-Operation plan jointly; HR lead owns theme and posts.

New Year Party

Yearly · 2026 held after Lunar New Year

Key steps

  • Pre Theme, invitation, backdrop
  • Pre Lucky-draw gifts + Star of the Year
  • In Lucky draw, recognition, CEO sharing
  • Post Wrap-up + recap
Budget modelHR per-head VND line × headcount per office.
RolesRecognitions: leaders choose, HR compiles, Leadership approval.

Company Birthday

Every year on 20/9

Key steps

  • Pre Theme + hashtags (2025 “Fourth & Forward”)
  • Pre Budget sheet + merch and gifts
  • In Company-wide celebration
Budget modelAdmin per-office USD line (bundled with September LFG) + HR per-head VND line.
RolesHR lead plans; Event support per office.

Women’s / Men’s Day

8/3 · 20/10 · 19/11

Key steps

  • Pre Vouchers (tested early), flowers, cake
  • Pre Decor finished the afternoon before
  • In Morning ceremony + lunch, gift set
  • Post Same-day photos from all offices, recap
Budget modelHR per-head VND line + Admin per-office USD line (+ contingency).
RolesHR lead: budget, vouchers, recap · Event support: decor, MC, photos.

Last Friday Get-Together

Monthly · last Friday, 5:00 PM

Key steps

  • Pre Monthly timeline; special Halloween and “LFG x Noel” editions
  • In Runs at each office
  • Post Photo recap + internal post
Budget modelAdmin per-office USD line; Christmas adds an HR per-head line.
RolesShoutouts: leaders choose, HR compiles, Leadership approval.

Recharge Time

About twice a year · Friday 18:30

Key steps

  • Pre Vendors, contract, deposit
  • Pre Internal + reminder posts
  • In Evening buffet in each region (fallback: same simple timeline)
RolesHR lead finds vendors; Admin supports contract and deposit.

Workshop series

Ad hoc · 30–60 min · hybrid

Key steps

  • Pre Announcement + registration form
  • Pre Collect attendee questions for the speaker; IT setup
  • In In the office or on Google Meet
  • Post Thank-you + recap
RolesHR lead: overall, form · Event support: reminders, IT, photos.

03 · Lessons learned

Small habits that prevented big problems

01

Decor the day before

Finish decor the afternoon before the event.

02

Plan B per region

Keep a local backup vendor in each region.

03

Same-day photos

Collect original photos from every office on the same day.

Where I look before I start

  • Notion keeps each event hub: plan, checklist, recap and photos.
  • Google Sheets tracks budget per head, headcount and registrations.
  • Canva holds the poster, backdrop and announcement templates.
  • Slack carries the announcement, reminders and same-day photos.
  • Google Forms collects registrations and post-event feedback.
